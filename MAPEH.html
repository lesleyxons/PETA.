What is it all about?

We created a jingle for our Eco-Safety campaign. We made our own lyrics and sang it using our real voices. We also used instruments from the original song, simple dance steps, and different props.

This video is the MAPEH part of our community campaign website. Our goal is to show the importance of keeping our surroundings clean and staying safe when dealing with waste and chemicals.

Our Props

Colorful Shirts

They represent a bright future. If we take care of our environment, we can have a colorful and better future.

Blue and Brown Shirts

Blue represents water while brown represents the earth and soil.

Caps

Caps help keep our heads covered from dust.

Handkerchiefs

They can be used to help clean surfaces.

Facemasks

They protect us from harmful germs, dust, smoke, and chemical fumes.

Gloves

Gloves protect our hands from dirt, germs, waste, and harmful chemicals.

Broom

A broom helps clean and sweep away trash and dirt.

Dustpan

A dustpan helps collect the trash after sweeping.

Chemicals

Chemicals are shown as examples of things that need to be handled carefully.

Headbands with Quotes

These have quotes about environmental problems and chemical safety rules.

Pompoms

These are additional props for our performance.

Why did we choose these props?

Every prop in our campaign has a purpose. Some represent the environment while others show simple ways that we can stay safe and keep our surroundings clean.

By using these props together with our jingle and dance, we can make our message easier and more fun to understand.
