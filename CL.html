DOMINICAN ADVOCACY MESSAGE

Truth We Share, Love is Everywhere

Our Advocacy Message

We, Group 4, as students, know that it can be hard and confusing to think about who we want to be in the future and how to handle different tasks in school and outside of school, which is our reality.

For us, living the Dominican way means building a life with faith and living with values through our daily lives. Our group made this Advocacy Message to show that trust in ourselves can be built and faith can make us stronger.

Our 4 Good Habits

For our Advocacy Message, we thoughtfully chose these four good habits as we are also Group 4.

1. Our Prayer

Prayer helps us take a break and have deep talks with God. Praying means knowing that God is with us and trusting Him with all our hearts.

Even a few quiet moments of prayer can bring us peace and guide us toward the right choices.

2. Our Unity

We want our classroom and community to feel like a home where everyone can feel safe and loved.

A classroom and community filled with love and respect for each other's differences can make everyone feel included. Mistakes should also be opportunities for improvement.

3. Our Service and Love

Service and love means showing up and turning our words into actions.

It means helping those who are in need with genuine intentions and without expecting something in return. Having the opportunity to provide care and support to people is already a blessing itself.

4. Our Faith

Faith gives us courage when we feel down and weak. It reminds us to do our best while also taking life one step at a time.

Through small and right choices every day, we can improve our faith.

Group 4's Ways of Spreading and Teaching the Word of God

As Dominicans, we can spread the Word of God through simple actions every day. We do not need to be perfect or make a big speech because small steps along the way can make the biggest changes if we value and love what we do.

We can start with the people around us. We, Group 4, cannot take action or teach others if we do not apply these values to ourselves first. We will continue to learn and teach at the same time so that we can grow even more.

1. A Helping Hand to Those in Need

A student notices their quiet classmate struggling with their activities, so they offer to help. They remind their classmate that they are not alone and that they are always there whenever they are needed.

2. Make an Advocacy Group

A group could leave encouraging notes for teachers, classmates, and the community. They could also give food to people who are hungry and help clean the environment.

3. Using Social Media the Right Way

We could post quotes and Bible verses online to share motivational and positive words.

We can also share stories about how faith helps people through difficult situations and encourages others to make more time for God.

4. Showing Kindness and Forgiveness

Instead of resenting people who have done us wrong, we should learn to forgive. All of us make mistakes because we are simply human and we are not perfect.

We should learn from our mistakes and improve ourselves so we do not make the same mistakes twice. Forgiving others is also an example of Catholic teaching.

5. Inviting Someone to Church

If a friend or someone we know is curious about Christianity, we could invite them to a youth group or church.

We could go to church together while learning and sharing the teachings of God.

Our Goal

WE AIM TO SERVE OUR PEOPLE AND COMMUNITY THROUGH GOOD ACTIONS.

Truth We Share, Love is Everywhere.
